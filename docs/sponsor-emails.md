firm, a dev shop/agency, and a bank/coworking space are the classic trio.

Numbers used are your real ones. Don't inflate them — the honesty is the pitch.

## Before sending — two blanks only you can fill

1. **The price.** I anchored $500/event or $1,500/quarter. Say it out loud; if you
   flinch, drop to $350/$1,000 — but do not go below that. The room is worth it.
2. **The workshop price** (for the rameshnuti.com page): my recommendation is
   **$2,500 half-day** for teams up to 20, in-person DFW or remote. Tell me the
   number and the workshop page copy gets finished the same day.

Rule of thumb for all three emails: send before you feel ready. The worst case is
a "no" from someone who already likes you.
